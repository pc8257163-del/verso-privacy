Privacy Policy

Verso – PDF Reader & Tools (com.verso.app) · Effective date: October 28, 2026

In short

Verso works on your device. It has no account or sign-in. Your PDF files are opened, edited and processed on your phone and are not uploaded to us or to any server run by us. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

Verso is an Android app published by YOUR DEVELOPER NAME (“we”, “us”, “our”). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at YOUR EMAIL.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or microphone data. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.

Your documents stay on your device. We never receive them.

3. Information stored on your device

To work properly, the app stores the following only on your device, in the app’s private storage:

  • a list of the PDF files found on your phone (file name, location, size, date, page count, thumbnails);
  • your reading progress, last page, bookmarks, favorites and folders you create;
  • annotations you make (highlights, underlines, drawings, text notes, signatures) and the signatures you choose to save;
  • your settings and reading statistics (for example reading time and streak);
  • temporary files created while a tool runs (for example merge or compress), which are removed after the task.

You can remove this data at any time with “Clear recent files” in Settings, by clearing the app’s storage in your phone settings, or by uninstalling the app.

4. Permissions the app uses

  • All files access / storage access: used only to find the PDF files on your phone and to open, edit and save them as you ask. The app does not read other kinds of files and does not send any file anywhere.
  • Internet and network state: not needed for reading or editing PDFs. They are used only in versions of the app that show ads (see section 6).
  • Advertising ID: used only in versions of the app that show ads (see section 6).

The app does not use your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

5. How your files are handled

  • All reading, searching, annotating, merging, splitting, compressing, watermarking, locking and unlocking happens on your device.
  • Files are shared with another app only when you tap Share or Save and choose where to send them.
  • The “Read aloud” feature uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. That engine is provided by your device or by another provider and is governed by its own privacy policy.
  • If you lock a PDF with a password, the password is not stored or sent anywhere. We cannot recover a forgotten password.

6. Advertising

Some versions of Verso may show ads. When ads are shown, they are provided by third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob and Unity Ads. These services may collect and use certain information to show and measure ads, for example your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system information, app interaction data and approximate location derived from the IP address. We do not give these services your documents or their contents.

Learn how these companies handle data:

  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Unity: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone). Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with anyone, except the advertising services described in section 6 in versions that show ads, and where we are required to do so by law.

8. Children

Verso is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Security

Because your files stay on your device, their security depends largely on your device. We recommend keeping your phone locked and updated. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal information, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control the data on your device as described in section 3. For data handled by advertising services, please use the controls described in section 6 or contact those services directly.
